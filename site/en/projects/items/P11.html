MetaGPT

Organize roles and standard deliverables into a collaborative software team.

Structured multi-agent software-development research system

Source snapshot: 2026-10-30 · Chinese original

1. Position in the system

MetaGPT organizes software-development collaboration through roles, actions and SOPs. Its important idea is structured requirements, design and implementation deliverables rather than simply making several models speak in sequence.

Best fit
Studying role/action/SOP-based collaboration, with paper settings and later product versions kept distinct.
Boundary
Scientific object contracts, external jobs and independent verification are not supplied by software-team roles.
Relation to other systems
Compare structured handoffs and role control with other frameworks rather than equating standard role names with scientific quality.

2. Mechanism at a glance

  1. Define roles and SOPs
  2. Execute actions and deliver artifacts
  3. Iterate through collaboration

Components and responsibilities

ComponentResponsibility
RolesDefine responsibilities, actions and consumed messages. Role boundaries determine modification authority and required inputs.
Actions and artifactsActions produce concrete outputs. Downstream roles should inspect designs, code or tests instead of trusting an upstream completion statement.
Teams and SOPsTeams organize collaboration and SOPs define expected handoffs. A fixed role sequence may fit a task family without generalizing to open scientific computation.

3. Follow a real run

  1. Convert a user requirement into staged team tasks.
  2. Execute role actions with the needed context.
  3. Pass structured artifacts and messages to downstream roles.
  4. Test final outputs and retain failures and rework.

State and recovery

Keep role configuration, messages, action outputs and revision history. Trace shared content to its source so an incorrect artifact is not repeatedly reinforced.

Deployment and dependencies

Distinguish research code, paper version, later optimization work and commercial products. Pin models, actions, tasks and environment, not just role names.

4. Tradeoffs and scientific use

Standard handoffs clarify collaboration, but fixed SOPs may overconstrain open-ended research. Domain objects, evidence and stopping conditions remain necessary.

Structural-biology adaptation

Map software deliverables to analysis plans, executable configurations and verification reports while deciding where feedback-driven branching is needed.

Practice task

Map software-team deliverables to an analysis plan, executable configuration and checking report. Identify which structural tasks fit a fixed SOP and which require observation-driven decisions.

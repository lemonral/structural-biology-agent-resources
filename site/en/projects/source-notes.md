This edition covers all 39 records in `site/projects/project_depth.json`: 20 frameworks, 14 application/scientific-integration systems and 5 infrastructure projects.

Every record retains its ID, source URLs, inspected code-entry metadata and original source date. The English prose translates and edits the original notes for readability, retaining mechanisms, components, run flow, state, deployment conditions, tradeoffs, practical exercises and reading focus. It does not constitute a new deployment, source audit, load test or benchmark reproduction.

Archive notices and migration statements describe the original source snapshot. Consult the linked official source for later changes. The Chinese catalogue and every corresponding original detail page remain linked explicitly as Chinese originals.

Scientific Agents Survey

Towards Scientific Intelligence: A Survey of LLM-based Scientific Agents

First public: Not recorded in source index · See publication record · See publication record

This scientific-agent survey maps Planner, Memory, Action Space and Verifier mechanisms. It is a design and reading framework rather than a unified implementation or an experiment proving every module combination superior.

Research problem

Scientific-agent literature mixes planning, memory, actions and evidence sources in ways that make broad claims of reliability difficult to compare.

How the mechanism works

  1. Separate prompt-driven from learned planning and distinguish instructions, deliberation, search, roles and programmatic structures.
  2. Map historical context, external and internal knowledge alongside actions such as retrieval, code, tools and environmental control.
  3. Classify self-correction, multi-role criticism, expert involvement and computation as different verification mechanisms, then relate them to research-task benchmarks.

Study setting and reported evidence

The archived v3 provides mechanism chapters, representative references, appendix mappings and teaching examples. Its benchmark map organizes coverage and evaluation levels, not directly comparable raw scores.

Source coverage: Original guide checked against the full text. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

Categories overlap and do not determine physical software boundaries. Cross-paper claims inherit differing models, tools and budgets; a verifier role does not itself guarantee scientific truth.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Use the four dimensions to identify missing state, capabilities or acceptance in a structural-agent design. Let actual task failures motivate implementation decisions rather than copying a generic survey diagram as a validated blueprint.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Track the survey version, definitions and original references. Inspect each cited study's evidence before treating a conceptual category as a measured reliability mechanism.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

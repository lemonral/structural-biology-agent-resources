R065 · Scientific systems and structural biology

Structural Refinement

AI agent supervision of structural model building and refinement

First public: 2026-10-08 · Preprint · bioRxiv · 2026-10-08

This structural-refinement study positions a general coding agent as a supervisor of mature scientific packages: prepare inputs, execute programs, inspect validation and select follow-up operations. It introduces no new refinement engine.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

Global quality improvements can conceal local damage or unresolved chemical identity, so accepting the newest model is an inadequate policy.

How the mechanism works

  1. Prepare software-specific inputs and run established modeling, refinement and validation tools with versions and parameters recorded.
  2. Retain parent and candidate models and inspect fit, geometry and the modified region jointly, allowing rejection or rollback.
  3. Deliver change reports with original coordinates, proposed edits, supporting evidence, rejected candidates and unresolved ligand/ion/modification assignments.

Study setting and reported evidence

The preprint presents 24 cryo-EM/X-ray cases under specified researcher-guided configurations. It records cases where global indicators improve while local quality worsens; it is not a comprehensive controlled comparison with all agents or fixed pipelines.

Limits and interpretation

Selected cases and a particular model configuration limit generalization. Coordinates and density cannot uniquely determine every chemical component, even when geometry and fit look acceptable.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Make local change reports central deliverables, preserving parent models, evidence and unresolved questions. Require both data fit and geometry and retain alternatives when justified, with expert review of sample chemistry.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Reconstruct inputs, package versions and candidate lineage. Score local and global changes independently and preserve rejected actions instead of reporting only a final improved scalar.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

Search R1

Search-R1: Training LLMs to Reason and Leverage Search Engines with Reinforcement Learning

First public: 2025-03-12 · Conference publication · COLM 2025 · Main conference

Search-R1 trains a model to issue search requests during reasoning and consume returned evidence. Search results enter the trajectory as observations, but their tokens are masked out of policy-learning losses.

Research problem

Inference-only retrieval augmentation does not necessarily teach a model when to search, how to revise a query or how to use the result.

How the mechanism works

  1. Pause generation at a search request, execute it and append results before continuing the trajectory.
  2. Distinguish model-generated action tokens from externally supplied text, excluding the latter from policy-gradient calculations.
  3. Use question-answer outcomes with PPO or GRPO to improve search and reasoning behavior without manually labeling every query chain.

Study setting and reported evidence

Experiments cover seven QA datasets, model sizes, retrievers and training settings. The archived notes avoid quoting one aggregate gain because abstract and body summaries differ.

Source coverage: Original guide checked against the full text. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

A correct answer can coexist with unnecessary search or unsupported citations. Learned behavior depends on the index, ranking and returned context, which may differ from open-web deployment.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Evaluate literature/manual retrieval separately from coordinate computation. A trained retrieval policy should preserve source versions, queries and citation-support checks rather than receiving reward only for answer matching.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Fix corpus and retriever snapshots, result lengths and request/response boundaries. Audit the observation mask, compare retrieval baselines under matching conditions and measure path quality as well as answer accuracy.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

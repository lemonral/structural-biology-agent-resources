ProteinGeoBench

ProteinGeoBench: Frontier Structural-Biology Agents Are Fluent at Lookup but Lost in Geometry

First public: 2026-10-19 · Preprint · arXiv · 2026-10-19 v1

ProteinGeoBench diagnoses the gap between structural-information lookup and coordinate computation, while auditing effects of execution configuration. Different experiment subsets cannot establish one universal ordering of model, harness, networking and task effects.

Research problem

A strong total score can hide geometry failures, partial credit and unstable rankings across repeated samples.

How the mechanism works

  1. Separate metadata, sequence checks, numerical geometry and conceptual tasks, retaining deterministic rewards and the smaller model-judged portion separately.
  2. Compare matched harness/access conditions where available, distinguishing native-CLI changes, domain blocking and complete offline runs from different batches.
  3. Audit repeats, submitted numbers and partial-credit rules; use additional numerical tasks to reduce floor effects and examine ranking stability.

Study setting and reported evidence

The common main comparison uses 130 tasks. Top models' mean-reward intervals overlap, and numerical tasks disproportionately contribute shared weak cases. Native-harness, networking, reasoning-budget and repeat experiments use selected subsets with distinct endpoints.

Limits and interpretation

Subset selection, batch differences and bundled harness changes prevent simple causal decomposition. Mean reward is not accuracy; partial credit and judge disagreement affect interpretation without invalidating every deterministic test.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Report structural-agent performance by task family and save atom selections, executed code and one explicit numerical answer. Align files, access, harness and budgets, using independent deterministic geometry checks.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Retain task subsets, grading batches, infrastructure-failure handling and paired uncertainty estimates. Treat small total-score leads and cross-model correlations cautiously instead of claiming stable scientific-capability rankings.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

R056 · Scientific systems and structural biology

MutexaGPT

MutexaGPT: an intuition-to-design translator for physics-based enzyme engineering

First public: 2026-06-19 · Journal publication · Nature Computational Science · 2026-09-10

MutexaGPT translates qualitative research intuition into explicit computational observables, simulation plans and explanations. It studies language-to-physics translation rather than acting as a direct general predictor of enzyme function.

Research problem

A researcher's qualitative objective may be incomplete or poorly aligned with the physical quantity a computational workflow can actually measure.

How the mechanism works

  1. QuestionAnalyzer checks relevance and completeness, clarifying the object, goal and missing conditions before computation.
  2. WorkPlanningBoard maps the request to proxy metrics and an EnzyHTP-readable configuration, keeping methodological assumptions explicit.
  3. Execution collects computational artifacts, while ResultExplainer reports observations, rankings and limitations separately from experimental materials.

Study setting and reported evidence

Role-level evaluations compare prompt and self-check configurations for relevance and completeness. Separate retrospective and prospective scientific cases compare computational observables with corresponding experimental endpoints.

Limits and interpretation

A relevant, complete plan can still choose inadequate sampling or inappropriate physical approximations. A useful proxy in one system does not guarantee transfer to other environments or functions.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Represent research goals, proxy metrics and methodological assumptions as separate objects, review their alignment and only then submit computation. The transfer is a design proposal, not proof of stable translation for every structural task.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Reconstruct role evaluation separately from end-to-end scientific cases. Preserve the distinction between existing experimental comparisons and newly performed experiments, without treating role success as scientific success.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

R080 · Scientific systems and structural biology

Coscientist (Gomes)

Autonomous chemical research with large language models

First public: Not recorded in source index · Journal publication · Nature · 624 · 2023

The Gomes-group Coscientist connects literature and equipment-document retrieval, code execution and laboratory automation for controlled chemistry tasks. It is a separate work from Google's later hypothesis-ranking Co-Scientist.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

A laboratory assistant must translate knowledge and technical instructions into executable operations while responding to measurements rather than merely proposing experiments.

How the mechanism works

  1. A central planner selects search, documentation, code or experiment interfaces and uses their returns to decide the next step.
  2. Separate general scientific information from device/API documentation and match instructions to actual versions, permissions and equipment state.
  3. Use executable interfaces and measurement feedback, retaining logs, physical observations and model interpretation as distinct evidence.

Study setting and reported evidence

The Nature 2023 paper presents six task categories involving knowledge, documentation, automation and feedback analysis. Task-level cases support semi-autonomous operation in particular prepared environments, not universal experimental autonomy.

Source coverage: Original guide checked against the full text. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

Equipment, material conditions and automation interfaces require substantial expert preparation. Search answers, successful calls, experimental artifacts and optimization outcomes establish different capabilities.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Adopt the separation of documentation, execution interfaces and measurements for scientific-software integration. Any future facility connection needs its own permissions, human-review points and acceptance scope.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Reconstruct the prepared environment and actual measurements, identify human contributions and evaluate each task's endpoint separately. Do not import Google's hypothesis-ranking evidence into this paper's automation claims.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

Keep the identities distinct

R025 · Google Co-Scientist

Google Co-Scientist iteratively generates, critiques, ranks and evolves research hypotheses under scientist guidance. It is distinct from the 2023 Gomes-group Coscientist experimental-automation system.

Compare mechanisms

R070 · ChemCrow

ChemCrow connects chemistry tools to a reasoning–action–observation loop. Its comparison between expert and model evaluation shows that fluent scientific narratives can still yield incorrect results.

R022 · Scientific systems and structural biology

Virtual Lab

The Virtual Lab of AI agents designs new SARS-CoV-2 nanobodies

First public: 2024-11-12 · Journal publication · Nature · 646:716–723 · 2025-07-29

Virtual Lab organizes interdisciplinary research through a principal-investigator role and specialist meetings, with human researchers guiding and implementing computation and experiments. It is a human–AI research case rather than an unattended laboratory.

Research problem

Research planning requires integrating specialized perspectives, criticism and real-world constraints while preserving a scientist's direction.

How the mechanism works

  1. A coordinator selects discussion topics and specialist roles around an explicit research objective.
  2. Team or individual meetings produce proposals, criticism and integrated next steps; agreement among roles remains discussion rather than independent evidence.
  3. Researchers supply high-level feedback, manage computations, conduct experiments and interpret their outcomes.

Study setting and reported evidence

The Nature paper reports a specific computational and experimental nanobody project. Project artifacts and experimental measurements support the case, but the notes identify no broad, matched-budget benchmark isolating the architecture's contribution.

Limits and interpretation

A successful project combines models, existing software and researcher expertise. Different roles using related models can repeat correlated misconceptions.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Use structured meetings to review analysis plans, tool assumptions and evidence gaps. Record whether each conclusion comes from a computation, publication or experiment, and retain human confirmation of objects and key interpretations.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Reconstruct actual human interventions and computational/experimental contributions, not only transcripts. Separate meeting mechanisms from candidate testing and avoid attributing laboratory work performed by people to autonomous model execution.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

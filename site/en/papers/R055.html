R055 · Scientific systems and structural biology

StructAgent

StructAgent: Orchestrating Cryo-EM Model Building and Refinement with a Multi-Agent LLM System

First public: 2026-05-18 · Preprint · bioRxiv · 2026-05-18

StructAgent separates literature/structural reasoning in Maria from local execution in Annika, retaining expert approval for substantial model changes. The paper presents auditable cryo-EM orchestration and evidence packages in three cases.

Research problem

Large structure-building projects need domain reasoning without losing file state, execution failures and the justification for modifying a model.

How the mechanism works

  1. Maria plans or revises strategy while Annika handles calls, files and recovery, exchanging focused consultations rather than sharing every log.
  2. Skills and knowledge sources specify inputs, checks and recovery; significant strategy, ligand or ion changes require user approval.
  3. Organize map fit, geometry and local chemical checks independently, retaining uncertainty where evidence is weak.

Study setting and reported evidence

Cases include a 64-chain proteasome refit, metal-site auditing and an ongoing ligand task. The first lacks half-maps and uses full-map validation; documented recoveries across cases do not establish a general 100% recovery rate.

Source coverage: Original guide checked against the full text. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

Refining and validating against the same map risks overfitting. Cases do not isolate two-agent superiority, and some data or full traces are not publicly reproducible.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Link every structural change to its reason, evidence and approval, allowing 'undetermined' outcomes. Reproduce public limited workflows before prospective comparisons; auditable logs and correct conclusions are distinct.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Track initial/reference models, map availability, local validators, failures and human approvals. Identify this cryo-EM StructAgent explicitly, and do not conflate it with unrelated similarly named agent papers.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

Plan Caching

Agentic Plan Caching: Test-Time Memory for Fast and Cost-Efficient LLM Agents

First public: 2025-06-17 · Conference publication · NeurIPS 2025 · Main Track

Agentic Plan Caching reuses procedural templates extracted from successful trajectories and adapts them to new context. It caches plans, then reruns them, instead of returning a previous answer tied to old data.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

Repeated task intents can waste large-model planning effort even when their entities, quantities and current observations differ.

How the mechanism works

  1. Extract high-level intent keywords with a small model and retrieve plan templates while reducing entity-name interference.
  2. Adapt cache hits to current inputs using a lightweight planner; use a larger planner on misses and continue observing during execution.
  3. Filter successful trajectories and remove contextual specifics to create compact templates, rather than storing full histories indiscriminately.

Study setting and reported evidence

FinanceBench, QASPER, TabMWP, AIME, GAIA and different agent workloads compare high-quality/low-cost settings, semantic caches and full-history reuse. Reported cost and latency reductions are not uniformly quality-neutral.

Limits and interpretation

Hit rates depend on workload repetition. Answer or model-judge metrics do not detect structural identity, units or applicability mistakes.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Cache accepted analysis skeletons with object types, preconditions and tool versions. Recompute structural metrics on current inputs; a cache hit must not bypass scientific checks or justify stale artifacts.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Measure retrieval/adaptation overhead, misses and end-to-end cost under realistic intent distributions. Separate cached templates from cached results and evaluate changed objects and tool versions explicitly.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

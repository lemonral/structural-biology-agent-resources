R063 · Scientific systems and structural biology

ELMS

Evaluator-in-the-Loop Monte Carlo Tree Search via LLM Agents for Motif Scaffolding in Protein Design

First public: 2026-10-02 · Preprint · arXiv · 2026-10-02

ELMS lets local evaluator evidence guide subsequent tree search, with an LLM interpreting failures and selecting constrained operations. Numerical evaluation, hard constraints and tree control remain programmatic responsibilities.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

A scalar success label gives too little information to decide which candidate branch or permitted modification deserves additional computation.

How the mechanism works

  1. Persist each candidate, mapping, parent, entering action and cached structural measurements as tree state.
  2. A critic summarizes external evidence while a policy selects from a frozen operation library; software executes constraints and independent measurements.
  3. MCTS allocates evaluations between exploration and promising branches, then returns candidates using predefined task metrics.

Study setting and reported evidence

GeomMotif and MotifBench computational experiments include matched-budget ablations and budget variation. Geometry, self-consistency and novelty criteria differ by task; final selection uses the same evaluator family optimized during search.

Limits and interpretation

Optimizing a validator can overfit its proxies. A small final submission may hide many internal evaluations, so output count is not total computation.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Represent structural validation as multiple evidence items from independent programs and use them to guide bounded next actions. Check for evaluator overfitting and retain every internal cost without reproducing a design procedure.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Fix operations, constraints and evaluator definitions before search. Separate search feedback, final selection and independent tests, and compare total evaluation budgets rather than final candidate counts.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

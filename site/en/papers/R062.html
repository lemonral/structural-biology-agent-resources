R062 · Evaluation, security and research maps

BIABench

BIABench: Evaluating AI agents on real-world bioimage analysis tasks

First public: 2026-09-28 · Preprint · arXiv · 2026

BIABench reconstructs end-to-end analysis from raw biological images and published scientific results. It scores final artifacts separately from the narrated process to test whether agents compute correct results consistently.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

A plausible analysis report can conceal an incorrect segmentation, measurement or other scientific output.

How the mechanism works

  1. Provide raw images, research questions and reference outcomes across modalities, requiring actual file processing rather than small-image visual QA.
  2. Compare artifacts with domain ground truth using objective result metrics, while a VLM separately assesses method and quality-control process against expert rubrics.
  3. Repeat runs and stratify two-dimensional, three-dimensional and temporal tasks to distinguish coverage from stability.

Study setting and reported evidence

The official abstract describes sixteen reconstructed tasks and repeated evaluations of general and biology-focused agents. The original review did not obtain the full paper, so detailed baseline tables and budgets were not reverified.

Source coverage: Original guide has limited source-version access. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

A small reconstructed suite does not cover every bioimage project, and the notes flag author involvement in some evaluated systems. VLM process judgments can miss invisible numerical errors.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Give map processing, structural comparison and trajectory statistics objective reference artifacts and repeated complete runs. A well-written report is review material, not a substitute for scientific-result scoring.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Begin with the linked abstract and full methods before quantitative comparison. Preserve separate process and outcome tracks and disclose reference construction, repeats and potential evaluation conflicts.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

Complementary benchmarks

R050 · BioAgent Bench

BioAgent Bench evaluates bioinformatics workflows through explicit inputs, required steps and output files, adding corruption, distractor and prompt-bloating tests. Workflow completion is necessary but insufficient for scientific reliability.

Domain evaluation

R079 · ProteinGeoBench

ProteinGeoBench diagnoses the gap between structural-information lookup and coordinate computation, while auditing effects of execution configuration. Different experiment subsets cannot establish one universal ordering of model, harness, networking and task effects.

R001 · Reasoning, action and orchestration

CoT

Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models

First public: 2022-01-28 · Conference publication · NeurIPS 2022 · Main Track

Chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting places a question, intermediate natural-language steps and an answer in each few-shot demonstration. It studies reasoning prompts rather than an agent that executes tools.

Research problemMechanismEvidenceLimitsImplementationReproductionReading connectionsSources

Research problem

Direct question–answer examples often omit the relations needed for arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic problems. CoT tests whether a different example format can elicit existing capabilities without changing model weights.

How the mechanism works

  1. Expand each demonstration into question, reasoning steps and answer, showing how conditions connect to intermediate calculations.
  2. Let the same model generate intermediate text before its answer. No calculator, retriever or independent validator corrects these steps.
  3. Compare model sizes, example authors, example order and prompt variants to separate reasoning structure from merely longer generation.

Study setting and reported evidence

The paper evaluates final-answer accuracy on arithmetic datasets such as GSM8K and SVAMP, plus commonsense and symbolic tasks, using LaMDA, GPT-3 and PaLM configurations. Larger models benefit across several settings; smaller models are less consistent.

Limits and interpretation

Readable explanations do not establish faithful internal reasoning or the truth of every intermediate statement. Results depend on model scale and demonstrations.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Use explicit reasoning to organize a proposed structural analysis, then delegate distances, angles and sequence mapping to verifiable tools. Generated reasoning is not evidence that coordinate calculations ran.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Hold questions, model and demonstrations fixed. Record decoding, example order and answer extraction; compare prompts with and without intermediate steps. Interpret the original NeurIPS 2022 setting separately from later zero-shot or self-consistency methods.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

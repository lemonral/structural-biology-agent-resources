R073 · Scientific systems and structural biology

PaperQA2

Language agents achieve superhuman synthesis of scientific knowledge

First public: Not recorded in source index · See publication record · See publication record

PaperQA2 repeatedly chooses literature search, evidence processing, citation traversal and answering for scientific synthesis. Its human-comparison claim is metric-specific: LitQA2 precision exceeds the comparison group, while overall accuracy is not significantly higher.

Research problem

Literature questions require sufficient supported evidence, and answering selectively changes the tradeoff between correctness among answered questions and total coverage.

How the mechanism works

  1. Search papers and traverse citations, repeating retrieval when the evidence state remains inadequate.
  2. Retrieve text chunks, rerank and summarize them, then answer using the highest-ranked evidence.
  3. Attach sources and extend to sectioned synthesis or claim support/contradiction review, keeping insufficient evidence distinct from positive counterevidence.

Study setting and reported evidence

LitQA2 compares repeated system runs with nine biology-trained humans: precision is 85.2% versus 73.8%, while accuracy is 66.0% versus 67.7%; the system abstains on some questions. Citation audits and contradiction review still find unsupported or unconfirmed machine claims.

Limits and interpretation

Abstention can raise precision while reducing coverage. Sources may support different conditions, so apparently different findings are not automatically logical contradictions.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Attach original passages and computational artifacts to structural interpretations. Label missing support and contradictory evidence separately; literature support does not replace coordinates or map evidence for the current sample.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Report precision, accuracy, abstention and retrieval together. Preserve source versions and inspect claim-level citation support, including context lost during summarization.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

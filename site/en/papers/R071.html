PDAgent: An LLM-Driven Autonomous Agent Framework Towards In Silico Protein Design via Directed Mutation

First public: Not recorded in source index · Conference publication · ICML 2026 · Main conference · PMLR 306:95170–95196

PDAgent uses a natural-language objective to guide iterative computational candidates around a template. Reported outcomes concern proxy constraints and predicted structural confidence, not experimentally established function or stability.

Research problem

A controller must translate a qualitative goal into comparable conditions and use computational feedback without silently changing what the goal means.

How the mechanism works

  1. Structure the request into objectives and conditions and select a starting point from existing resources.
  2. Separate THINK, PLAN, ACT, OBSERVE and REFLECT stages, tracking predictions and history across iterations.
  3. Use external programs for structure/property proxies and screen constraints before continuing or returning results.

Study setting and reported evidence

A hundred model-assisted tasks compare prompting, specialized methods and history/iteration/stage ablations. Metrics separately measure valid-sequence generation, ESMFold confidence and computational-constraint satisfaction. A second predictor on thirty candidates remains computational corroboration.

Source coverage: Original guide checked against the full text. This English edition translates the archived review and does not claim a new independent reproduction.

Limits and interpretation

The manuscript has no wet-laboratory validation. Isoelectric-point matching is not measured pH stability; template priors and unequal starting points or loop budgets also affect comparisons.

Implications for a structural-biology agent

Editorial design inference, distinct from the paper's measured result.

Audit whether the requested scientific concept and measurable proxy are actually equivalent before optimizing it. Distinguish confidence, geometry and biological truth; a feedback loop cannot fix a misdefined acceptance criterion.

Reproduction and implementation checks

Preserve templates, all iterations, metric definitions and category weighting. Compare macro/micro constraint scores and controller effects under matched starting structures and computational budgets.

Reading connections

Use the paired mechanisms to locate what changes between systems: information access, control, memory, training, execution or acceptance.

A suggested reading sequence

  1. Agent decisions and feedback: start with ReAct, planning, tool execution and state.
  2. Research mechanisms: compare memory, search, orchestration, optimization and collaboration under their own assumptions.
  3. Scientific applications: distinguish knowledge assistance, computational workflows, experimental choice and structural determination.
  4. Engineering: specify contracts, run reference tasks, persist state and validate actual artifacts.
  5. Experiments: compare against fixed workflows with matched tools/budgets, frozen scoring and fault injection.

What this archive represents

This is a research and design library. Capability maps describe intended scope; adapters, validators and reference tasks establish actual availability. Published cases, software catalogues, teaching implementations and proposed experiments support different kinds of claims.

The English guides are editorial translations of the archived Chinese research notes, with source-specific detail and links to the originals. Original verification dates and publication versions remain attached to the relevant sources. Historical Chinese courses and decks remain available in the archive; the current English presentation and development documents are linked above.

Language editions describe the same design baseline. They do not create another product version or a newly deployed scientific agent.
